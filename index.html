        Import graves

        Paste rows copied from your spreadsheet, or choose a .tsv/.csv file. Needed columns: Long and Lat, and either a WikiTree ID or names. Optional: FindAGrave, FamilySearch, Image link (a web address, WikiTree or anywhere else), Given Names, Surname, Maiden, Birth, Death, Section, Plot, Monument (the heading on the stone), Reference, Notes. A row with names but no WikiTree ID is kept as a burial that can be given its ID later. People who share a headstone share its Long, Lat and image.

        (only works when this app is served from wikitree.com)

            Cemetery file (snapshot and overlay)

            For the person who looks after this app's folder on the apps server: a saved copy, used when WikiTree can't be read, which also keeps the map overlay. It goes in cemeteries/. It is not needed for the cemetery to be found.

            Index entry

            For the person who looks after this app's folder on the apps server: an entry for cemeteries/index.json. Not needed for a cemetery whose plot-map pages are in its category and in Plotting Pages, which is listed automatically.

            Plot map overlay

            Lay a scanned cemetery plot map over the satellite view, and/or add section outlines and landmarks. Both are optional and saved in the cemetery file.
