Update: Cemetery Map version 0.6.1. Thank you to everyone who has tried it. Here is what has changed since my first post. The version is now shown at the bottom right of the map.

What's new

  • Burials without a WikiTree profile yet. A row in a plot-map table with a name (Given Names, Surname, Maiden, Birth, Death, Notes) but no WikiTree ID is now kept: it is listed, shown on its headstone with everyone else who shares it, and written back to the table when you publish. Use Show: Needs WikiTree ID to find them.
  • Identify, Locate, Map. In the list, Locate adds a location, Identify adds the WikiTree ID once the person has a profile, and Map shows them on the map. Clicking a person who has no location yet now shows their details and headstone photo.
  • Headstone photos can be stored anywhere. A photo does not have to be on WikiTree. A Google Drive share link works too, as long as the file is shared with "anyone with the link".
  • A quieter Data check. Problems are grouped per Space page with a count and a link, a photo shared by people whose locations are more than 10 m apart links to the photo's page, and so does a marker that is far outside the cemetery. Living people are always left out. Unknown headstones are shown as grey markers.
  • Large cemeteries that keep one Space page per section are read page by page, and the WikiTree logo and credit are now on the map.

Still looking for testers

  • Do you have a Space page that holds WikiTree IDs, GPS coordinates and headstone images? Please open your cemetery in the app and tell me whether your table is found and looks right on the map.
  • Do you have a cemetery where not everyone has a WikiTree profile yet? Try the new name and date columns. The last rows of the Fairview table show the layout, and the example map is here: Fairview Presbyterian Cemetery.

When you report something, please include the version number from the map. The full description, with screenshots, is on Space:Cemetery_Map. Photo uploads from a phone are being tested in the mobile image upload post, so please leave those there.
