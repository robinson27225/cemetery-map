Update: Cemetery Map version 0.8.0. The big change is that cemeteries that have a plot map now show up in the app on their own, so there is no list to keep up to date.

What's new

  • Ready-made maps are found automatically. Open Find a cemetery… and the cemeteries that have a plot map are listed at the top under Ready-made maps. They are also marked map ready wherever they appear in the place lists. The list is built from Category:Plotting_Pages, so a newly categorised cemetery appears within a few minutes.
  • The Publish window shows the category lines to add to your plot-map page.

How to get your cemetery on the list

Put every plot-map page for your cemetery (including one page per section, if you split it up) in two categories:

  1. the cemetery's own WikiTree category, which is how the map finds the page's data, and
  2. Category:Plotting_Pages, which is how the app finds out that the cemetery has a map.

For example, on the Fairview plot-map page: [[Category:Fairview_Presbyterian_Cemetery,_Raton,_New_Mexico]] and [[Category:Plotting_Pages]]. If a cemetery shows its profiles but nothing on the map, a missing category on the plot-map page is the first thing to check.

Still looking for testers

  • Do you have a Space page that holds WikiTree IDs, GPS coordinates and headstone images? Add the two categories, then open your cemetery in the app. Does it appear under Ready-made maps, and does the map look right?
  • Cemeteries with many burials on each monument, or burials without a WikiTree profile yet, are also very welcome.
