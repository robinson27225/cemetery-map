Hi WikiTreers,

Cemetery Map is a WikiTree App that puts the people buried in a cemetery on a satellite map, with each headstone's photo and links to WikiTree, Find a Grave and FamilySearch. Thanks to the testers' reports it has changed a lot since my first post, so this is a new post about version 0.8.6, and I am still looking for testers.

What is new

  • Ready-made maps find themselves. Open Find a cemetery… and the cemeteries that already have a plot map are listed at the top under Ready-made maps, and marked map ready wherever they appear in the place lists. The list is built from Category:Plotting_Pages, so nobody has to edit a list.
  • List by monument. One row per headstone: its plot number or reference, how many are buried there, and who. Big cemeteries open in this view. The plot number also shows beside the pin when you zoom in.
  • Burials without a WikiTree profile yet are kept: a row with names and dates but no WikiTree ID is listed and mapped, found with Show: Needs WikiTree ID, and can be given its ID later with Identify.
  • Large cemeteries that keep one Space page per section are read, including album-style tables, and so are pages with accents in their titles (like Cimetière de Sainte-Famille, Île d'Orléans, Québec).
  • Headstone photos can be stored anywhere, including a Google Drive share link. Unknown headstones are shown as grey markers, and living people are always left out.
  • The Data check is grouped per Space page with counts and links, so a work-in-progress cemetery does not produce a wall of text.

Starting a cemetery from Find a Grave

The WikiTree Browser Extension has a feature, Find A Grave Memorial Extractor by Miyako Jones (Jones-108375), that reads a Find a Grave cemetery's memorial list and downloads it as a CSV file, matching memorials with WikiTree profiles where it can. That is a ready-made list of everyone in the cemetery. Rearrange the columns to WikiTree ID, FindAGrave, FamilySearch, Long, Lat, Given Names, Surname, Maiden, Birth, Death, Notes (leave Long and Lat empty), paste it into Import data, and everyone is listed as a burial to locate. As people are placed and given their WikiTree IDs, the table you publish grows into the cemetery's plot map. I tried it with a list of 1,152 memorials from Fern Hill Cemetery, Aberdeen, Washington, and the importer read every row.

One limit: the extractor's list does not include the GPS location of each grave from Find a Grave, so the people arrive listed but not yet on the map, and their positions still have to be added in the app.

An idea for Hacktoberfest: a hybrid of Doug Foster's Find a Grave Tools (Python scripts that save Find a Grave pages and report their data to a spreadsheet) and Miyako's extractor would be a good project for anyone who would like to build something. The extractor already produces the memorial list and matches the WikiTree profiles; adding each grave's location to that CSV would give Cemetery Map a list that arrives already on the map. If you are interested, please say so in a comment.

Getting your cemetery on the list of ready-made maps

Put every plot-map page for your cemetery in two categories: the cemetery's own WikiTree category (that is how the map finds the page's data) and Category:Plotting_Pages (that is how the app learns that the cemetery has a map). The Publish window shows the exact two lines for your cemetery.

What I would love testers to try

  1. If you have a Space page with a table of WikiTree ID, GPS coordinates and a headstone image: add the two categories, then open your cemetery in the app. Does it appear under Ready-made maps, and do the headstones land in the right place with the right photo?
  2. If you have a cemetery with many burials on each monument, try the monument view and tell me whether the plot labels suit your data.
  3. Try the Find a Grave route above on a cemetery you know, and tell me where the steps were unclear.
  4. Pick someone, choose their headstone photo, place them on the map and click Publish…. Please paste onto a Space page of your own, not another project's page.

Please leave the photo upload itself to the mobile image upload post; this app starts after the photo is on WikiTree.

Good to know

  • Nothing is saved to WikiTree automatically. Publish only prepares text for you to paste, because apps cannot write to Space pages. Until then, your changes are stored only in your own browser.
  • Reading Space pages and names works while the app is open on wikitree.com, and WikiTree's access checks can occasionally refuse a request. Reloading after visiting a WikiTree page usually fixes it. Please tell me when it happens.
  • It is a beta, and I have only used it in a desktop browser, so it has not been tested on phones.

Feedback

When you report something, please include the version number shown at the bottom right of the map (like "Cemetery Map v0.8.6"). Please answer here, comment on the Space page, or send me a private message.
