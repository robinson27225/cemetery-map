Update: Cemetery Map version 0.7.0. This version came out of a test on a real cemetery with more than a thousand burials, so thank you to the tester who tried it. The version is shown at the bottom right of the map.

What's new

  • Pages with accents in their titles now work. The app was skipping the plot-map page of any cemetery whose title has an accent (for example Cimetière de Sainte-Famille, Île d'Orléans, Québec), so nothing was listed. That is fixed.
  • List by monument. A new List by menu shows one row per headstone instead of one per person: its plot number or reference, how many are buried there, and who. A big cemetery opens in this view. The people list now has a Show more button instead of stopping at 600.
  • Plot number on the map. A headstone's plot number shows beside its pin when you zoom in, and in its details. It comes from a Plot column if the table has one, otherwise from a short reference that everyone on that stone shares in the Notes column.
  • Publish reminds you about the category. The Publish window now shows the exact category line to add to the plot-map page.

One important thing: put the plot-map page in the cemetery's category

The map finds a cemetery's plot-map page through the cemetery's WikiTree category. If you create a plot-map page, add the cemetery's category to it ([[Category:Name of the cemetery]]). If a cemetery shows its profiles but nothing on the map, a missing category on the plot-map page is the first thing to check.

  • Do you have a cemetery with many burials on each monument, or burials that do not have a WikiTree profile yet? The monument view and the name columns are new, and I would like to know whether they suit your data.
